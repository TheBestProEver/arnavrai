BeaconA low-cost SOS wearable with satellite fallback for off‑grid emergency response

Nearly every personal safety tool assumes a working phone in your hand. Beacon is a one-button wearable with its own cellular link, GPS and a planned satellite fallback over 3GPP Release-17 NB-NTN. The poster tests whether that can be built from commercial cellular-IoT hardware and still sell for $50 to $100.

Standalone
One press reaches contacts over the device’s own LTE-M link. Modem verified on the nRF9160 dev kit; end-to-end alert in progress.
Satellite
The same short SOS goes directly to a satellite where there’s no cell coverage, using the nRF9151. Untested so far.
Cost
Hardware plus five years of connectivity at a $50 to $100 one-time price. Base cellular hardware estimated at $24 to $43 per unit.
